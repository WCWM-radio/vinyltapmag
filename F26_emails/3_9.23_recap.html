Updates for artists! Artist sign-up will start at our next meeting on Wednesday, September 30th. Here's how it will work:

How Artist Sign-Up Works
  • At meeting next week, we'll share a Google Sheet listing all of the articles going to print. Put your name next to the article you want to do art for — it's first come, first served.
  • Once you have an article, you and the writer will both meet with Andrew and Ilias. Meeting times are still TBD, but they'll be set by sign-up time.
  • Artists and writers should message each other to agree on which meeting time works best, since both of you need to be there.
  • Bring a rough idea of your plan for the art to the meeting, with sketches or mockups where applicable.

New Slack channels to join! Hop into whichever ones fit what you want to work on:

Slack Channels
  • #art-and-design — interested in doing art of any medium
  • #photographers — interested in taking photos at a photoshoot
  • #production-photoshoot — interested in planning photoshoot(s) and visual guides
  • #writers — writing an article or album review

How to join a channel: In the Slack app, tap the search button in the bottom right corner, type the channel name (e.g. #writers), open the channel, and click Join Channel at the bottom. On desktop, you can also click + Add channels → Browse channels in the left sidebar to see every channel and join from there.
